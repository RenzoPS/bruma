a waiver of rights and asks for no credit. They are recorded anyway because
"where did this photo come from?" should be answerable without searching for it
again.

## Why none of them is CC BY any more

Four of these five used to be **CC BY**, which does require visible credit, and
the site footer gave none. That is a licence violation, not a matter of taste:
while they were there, publishing the site meant publishing it wrong.

It was fixed by replacing them rather than by adding a credit line to the footer.
Both options were correct; this one was chosen because four photo credits in the
footer of a portfolio site weigh more than the photos do, and because replacing
removes the obligation instead of administering it. The replacements were picked
to keep the warm light and the wooden ground of the rest of the site, which is
what made the menu read as one piece.

If a new photo is ever added: **CC0 or generated, nothing else.** A CC BY image
means keeping a credit list current, and a list somebody has to remember to
update is a list that goes stale.
