# Prompts de Gemini — BRUMA

Cada prompt está completo y se copia solo. No hace falta agregarle nada.

Las imágenes van a `apps/web/public/estaciones/` con el nombre que dice cada
bloque. Están ordenados por importancia: si vas a generar solo algunas, hacé las
primeras.

## 1 · `tostadora.jpg` — la más importante

Es el argumento entero de la marca y abre la página.

```
A photorealistic photograph of a small-batch drum coffee roaster in operation inside a bright café, shot from a three-quarter front angle at eye level.

THE MACHINE: a compact commercial drum roaster in painted steel with a brass or chrome front plate. Its round glass sight window glows warm from the beans tumbling inside. An analog temperature gauge on the front. The trier tube protruding from the face. Some honest wear on the metal — this machine works every day.

THE SETTING: the roaster stands inside the café itself, not in a warehouse or factory. A large street-facing window behind it floods the frame with daylight. Blurred pale interior, a tiled wall, the hint of a counter edge.

LIGHT: strong natural daylight from a wide window, broad and diffused, soft open shadows. Cool to neutral white balance — bright and airy, NOT golden hour, NOT warm tungsten, NOT a dark moody interior. The sight window is the single warm point in an otherwise cool bright frame.

COLOR: pale cool background tones around #F2F3F0, brushed steel, light wood and smooth concrete surfaces.

FRAMING: vertical-friendly composition with headroom above the machine. Shallow depth of field on the background. Natural and documentary, not styled or advertised.

No text, no logos, no watermarks, no brand names, no recognizable faces.
```

## 10 · El logo

Último, cuando las fotos ya fijaron el mundo. Guardalo como `public/logo.svg` o
`logo.png` con fondo transparente.

```
A minimal logotype for a specialty coffee roastery called BRUMA.

THE WORDMARK: the word BRUMA set in a wide grotesque sans-serif, all capitals, tight letter spacing, heavy weight, optically even. Flat solid black on white. No gradient, no shadow, no outline, no three-dimensional effect, no texture.

THE MARK: beside or above the word, one small geometric symbol suggesting steam or mist — two or three simple curved strokes, drawn with the same stroke weight as the letterforms so the mark and the word read as a single object. Geometric and constructed, not hand-drawn, not calligraphic.

The mark must NOT be a coffee bean, NOT a cup, NOT a leaf, NOT a mountain, NOT a sun. Only abstract curved strokes suggesting rising steam.

Pure flat vector style on a plain white background, suitable for single-color printing. Nothing else in the frame — no mockup, no business card, no background scene.
```

**Qué revisar antes de darlo por bueno:** que funcione en un solo color, que la
marca de vapor tenga el mismo grosor de trazo que las letras, y que no haya
aparecido ni un grano ni una taza. Si Gemini te mete una taza o una hojita,
descartalo y pedilo de nuevo: es su default para café y no pertenece a este
mundo.

## 11 y 12 · `tueste-a.jpg` y `tueste-b.jpg` — la animación de scroll

Estas dos son **un par**: se funden una en la otra mientras el visitante
scrollea, así que **tienen que tener exactamente el mismo encuadre, la misma
luz y la misma bandeja**. Lo único que cambia es el color del grano.

**El orden importa:** generá primero la A. Después, para la B, **adjuntá la
imagen A** y usá el prompt de abajo, que le pide a Gemini partir de ella. Si
generás las dos por separado te van a salir con distinto encuadre y el crossfade
se va a ver como un corte, no como un tueste.

### 11 · `tueste-a.jpg` — el grano crudo

```
A photorealistic top-down photograph of raw green coffee beans spread in a single even layer on a wide shallow stainless steel cooling tray, shot from directly above, perfectly flat to the surface.

THE BEANS: unroasted arabica in dull sage-green, matte, with pale center cuts, filling the entire frame edge to edge in an even layer about one bean deep. Slightly irregular sizes and natural random arrangement.

LIGHT: broad diffused daylight from a large window on the left, soft open shadows between the beans, no hard specular highlights. Bright, airy, cool neutral white balance. NOT warm tungsten, NOT golden hour, NOT a dark background.

COLOR: pale cool tones around #F2F3F0 on the steel showing at the edges.

FRAMING: perfectly perpendicular top-down view, the tray filling the whole frame, horizontal 16:10 composition. Absolutely flat to the surface, no tilt, no angle.

No text, no logos, no watermarks, no brand names, no hands, no people.
```

### 12 · `tueste-b.jpg` — el mismo grano, ya tostado

**Adjuntá `tueste-a.jpg` junto con este prompt.**

```
Using the attached image as the exact reference, generate the same photograph with only one change: the coffee beans are now fully roasted.

KEEP IDENTICAL: the camera angle (perfectly top-down), the framing and crop, the tray, the lighting direction and quality, the white balance, the depth of field, and the overall arrangement and density of the beans in the frame. The composition must match the attached image as closely as possible so the two images can be cross-faded into each other.

CHANGE ONLY THE BEANS: they are now medium-dark roasted — deep cinnamon to chocolate brown, with a subtle oil sheen on the surface, slightly expanded and rounder than the raw beans, with the center cut now showing as a lighter line. They must read as unmistakably roasted coffee.

LIGHT: identical to the attached image — broad diffused daylight from the left, cool neutral white balance, soft open shadows.

No text, no logos, no watermarks, no brand names, no hands, no people.
```

**Qué revisar:** poné las dos imágenes una encima de otra y fijate que la bandeja
y los bordes coincidan. Si el encuadre se corrió, volvé a pedir la B insistiendo
en `keep the exact same camera angle and crop as the attached image`.
